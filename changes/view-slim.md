Removed

The library's own `Limits` struct, with its `Default` and its conversion to GridCraft's limits (record 0010, decision 2, step 6). `carrysheet::Limits` is now GridCraft's limits type, and `carrysheet::LIMITS` holds Carrysheet's values. Its fields have GridCraft's names, such as `work` and `seconds`, and the csv byte limit is no longer one of them. `Limits::default()` now means no limit, so a caller starts from `LIMITS`. A library caller that passes an oversized workbook to `Kernel::open_bytes` now reads "<path> is too large to open"; the code, the why and the fix are the same. Also removed from the library, unused in the workspace: `view::CellInfo` and `view::requested_sheet`. `carrysheet-cli` prints the same JSON, error codes and exit codes. The old code stays in the history of `main`, for example in `deca503` (`crates/carrysheet/src/lib.rs`, `crates/carrysheet/src/view.rs`).
